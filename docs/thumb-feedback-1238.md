# Prompt feedback for a thumb (1238)

A native thumb press immediately shows “Saving thumbs-up…” (or “Removing
thumbs-up…”) on that message and its active controls. This is local pending
truth: the aggregate count, own pressed state, reaction revisions, reading,
folding and New line remain confirmed Core facts. Another press while that
message is busy sends no second command.

The existing authority-fenced command and message-scoped reconciliation read
remain ordered. A current authoritative answer updates that message, its
folded thumb, the affected dossier's existing New count and the same Reader.
There is no full-feed reaction paint on local thumb completion. Confirmed
mark clearing, receipt-driven New-line placement and existing stack-deal
consequences retain their owners. Refusal/unknown notices keep their existing
classification; a failed read does not manufacture a confirmed reaction.
Late results from a retired page or authority cannot paint its successor.

Feed bar, reaction-count click, folded-row click/keys and Reader thumb share
the same pending owner. Go-ahead answers retain their existing behavior.

Rule9 orders focused shipping and empirical proof before the usual consumer
cycle. Flex's native action evidence must identify exact source, click,
pending DOM/frame, command/body, reconciliation and confirmed DOM intervals;
DOM events or frame callbacks alone are not pixel timestamps. The target is
under100ms click-to-pending feedback. Preserve unchanged-card identity and
reconcile refusal, failed/unknown result, late navigation and confirmed
read/stack/New-line effects in the later complete-file controls.
