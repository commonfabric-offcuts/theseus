# Reading-place rebuild (785)

Landing #11182 authorizes this rebuild after 838: current live product base,
focused quick tests adding no failures over live, no preview or browser run.
It supersedes the old preview and native-allocation workflow. The existing
785 piece uses Fable's agent-created topic, not a Tony-created topic.

The retained source is the arrival reading hold: an outside arrival above
the reading position absorbs its displacement. At document top an arrival
waits connected but hidden behind New above until a deliberate reading
destination or scrolling above its insertion point admits it. Writing, ordinary
card acts and unrelated layout retain their own movement. A continuous hold
must belong to a recorded arrival and cannot undo a later personal scroll.

Composition preserves the current inline reply, document-only New markers,
dossier New selection and confirmed-read rules. Existing source and checks
are retained; the rebuild must name the earlier paging, composer and synthetic
growth failures in code before it freezes. No earlier browser result is proof
of the rebuilt tree, and no native proof is claimed for this release.

## 1242 arrival offer count

New above shows the number of genuine retained arrival offers. Passive paints
prune stale entry/message/topic identities without measuring offered cards or
pretending to know their current viewport positions. A connected offer may
remain after scrolling; it retires when its actual activation reaches it, or
its source/entry ceases to be current. The count is not an unread total.
Activation retains reveal, top-arrival release, scroll/focus and reached-identity
fences. Hidden, retired, inaccessible or authority-held views cannot activate
an old offer. The separate arrival/writing hold owners remain unchanged.
The existing single message-column position read is not a per-card scan.
Focused checks execute the actual painter with card geometry that throws on
passive use; native action/traffic timing and geometry remain separate proof.
