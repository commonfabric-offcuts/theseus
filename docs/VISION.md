# Theseus: people and agents making useful things happen

Proposal, 12 November 2026. This branch is not the active operating policy.

Theseus turns human intention into useful, trustworthy results. People and
agents share context, combine different strengths, and act together. Agents
notice opportunities and pursue authorized work without waiting to be managed.

**Own a result. Make it real early. Learn from what happens. Finish the job.**

One person or agent owns each result through actual use. A hands-on peer brings
another way of seeing and helps expose mistakes. Both can make the work better;
neither is employed merely to move it between stages. Add people or machinery
when they shorten the path to a better result.

Work meets reality early: a usable preview, a working command, a decision with
evidence, or a completed act. We keep the evidence that matters and distinguish
what is demonstrated from what we hope is true. Speed and reliability both
count. Writing survives interruption; authority, privacy and attribution hold.

Theseus keeps shared knowledge, commitments and results durable. Its tools make
coordination cheaper as work grows. A board entry, a passing test or an agent's
confidence cannot substitute for the promised experience.

We build Theseus with Theseus and improve how we improve it. Its direction is
Common Fabric: people and agents creating and evolving useful systems on the
labs runtime, with its security and ownership model. Theseus earns that future
by being an effective workbench now.

When work repeatedly produces more coordination than progress, change the
approach. The [working charter](working-charter.md) makes this promise testable.
