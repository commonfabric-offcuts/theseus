# A submitted card becomes its confirmed card (1249)

The exact frozen submission is a local presentation, outside TopicViews and
messageEntries. It has no speculative message id, sequence, unread state or
reactions. It uses ordinary card author/word/reply/image renderers, with an
honest quiet pending mark. Topic writing rests before the bottom composer;
reply writing rests immediately under its actual reply parent.

Only a validated committed send plus the matching authorized canonical page
permits adoption. The canonical painter binds that existing outer card to
the real id and sequence. The server's body, author, relationships and files
win. Equal content keeps its word/image nodes; changed content is repainted
under the existing reading anchor. Confirmation never creates a second local
copy or writes speculative records to the authoritative map.

Unknown/refused submissions, Check confirmation and unchanged-successor
restoration stay with the existing editor/session owners. Image previews use
the original submission's frozen selection links and binding, never the
successor's selection. A retired topic/access/authority cannot adopt or publish
late bytes. Disposal releases the local visual resources, not journaled work.

Pointer/key inline replies, the bottom pencil and Reader share this owner.
Focused controls cover contents, position/node/focus identity, canonical
differences, refusal/unknown/later writing/images and authority/topic fences.
