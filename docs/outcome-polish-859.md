# Outcome page polish (859)

Notebook document titles occupy at most two lines, with an ellipsis and the
whole title in the tooltip and accessible name. Revision counts stay beside
the title's first line. The outcome's id is not drawn above its title.

The outcome Team uses the topic Team's markup, header placement, classes and
spacing, without outcome-only layout rules. Its bar ends at + Add. The Add
popover lists + Invite after the available names, with the same choice-pill
shape. AgentInvite remains the only invitation owner. Its name line closes
without sending on Escape, a completed outside press, or closure of Add.
Dragging a selection out does not dismiss it. Pending invitations keep their
exact request and completed prompts remain visible outside the popover.

Topic cards use the selected siderail background and accent title; hover and
keyboard focus remain distinguishable. The New topic pencil follows Topics
immediately. Topic pages retain their existing eyebrow and Team behavior.

Checks exercise the actual invitation and people handlers and inspect the
shared markup/CSS contract. They do not prove rendered geometry or gestures.
