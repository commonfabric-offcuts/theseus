# Review only different writing (931)

Review versions describes a known difference in the words, images, quotes or
recipients of a saved copy and the current field. Empty upload bookkeeping,
caret position, save counters, unknown ownership and a page cursor are not
writing differences. A save followed by more typing in the same draft is not
another version to choose. Compare readable writing before offering the pill;
its title names the first difference and the comparison opens with that copy.
Identical copies remain safely retained until the existing cleanup proves that
it can retire them. They do not ask the person to reconcile identical words.

Runtime changes, unreadable image ownership and unknown send outcomes retain
their existing write gates and recovery actions. An operational recovery action
must not claim that two versions differ when their contents are unknown.

Keeping current writing over an exact refused copy records a choice on that
copy under its writer lock and stamp guard. The choice never deletes writing,
bytes or attempts and never sends anything. A writing or image-choice change invalidates it. Proven release of retained bytes alone carries it forward, without changing writing or sends.
Within this document it applies only to the chosen writing cycle; after reload
it applies only when the canonical draft slot, authority and writing still match
the chosen field. Selected image work remains independently gated. Failure to
record the choice leaves the review outstanding.
