# Source-bound compose groups (775)

A compose obligation is the ordered inventory of `(phase, engine, name)` at an
exact product commit/tree. Independent groups partition that inventory; retained
completed phases belong to the same union, while partial rows are evidence, not
completed coverage. Mutant and restored controls inside a case are indivisible.

Prepare inventories without browser, fixture or product execution. A separate,
hashed harness delta implements exact identity selection and bounded cases.
Keep all seed/helper setup and the answering coordinator precondition. An empty
selection refuses. Never call a patched harness unchanged source. Record product,
instrument, wrapper and actual runtime separately, retaining original producer,
selector, collector, dispatch and raw false rows.

The deadline includes the original action, context close and reset barriers.
Timeout latches terminal failure, writes a case-identified red with source and
wall time, attempts bounded owned cleanup and ends the group. It never advances
to another case. A cleanup timeout stays unknown/false, including pending action
work, and leaves the remainder unfinished. Independent jobs use separate source
copies and fixture origins. Setup/finalization have their own deadlines.

Future plans expand native-compose into independent groups, each with the
installed 120 minute capacity, 5 minute reserve and 1.25 maximum observations;
unknown groups run solo. A changed inventory cannot inherit completion. The
collector requires one exact disjoint receipt per group and retains all raw reds.
Existing strict continuation/base-move formats are not extended by this proposal;
unsupported phase/group/instrument admission remains a literal contract gap.

The prepared train49 wrapper and evidence in checks/775-compose9480 are for
independent audit. No approval, dispatch, runtime cleanup or native pass follows
from preparation. Current trains remain untouched.
