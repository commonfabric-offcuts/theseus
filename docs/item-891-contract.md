# Your withdrawn question leaves your feed

A question you wrote and withdrew no longer shows to you in the feed or its
dossier. Other readers still see the question and its withdrawn mark. The rule
uses the existing viewer, author, ask intent and withdrawal fact; no new saved
hide state, permissions or record removal is introduced.

Replies remain under their canonical dossier, in its existing order. Grouping
uses the original records before choosing the visible messages, so a hidden
root or nested question does not split its replies into another dossier. A
supplemental dossier-head read cannot promote your withdrawn question back
into the tab, and a fold, Earlier messages, Only new or a star cannot reveal it.

The dossier keeps its explicit or agent-minted name and independent cover
metadata. With a hidden root and no such name, the existing reply-headed
fallback is “Dossier”. The generated Replying-to line omits an excerpt of your
withdrawn question known from the feed’s existing loaded records or dossier-head
cache, while keeping its author cue. A parent known only to Reader, or absent
from those feed records, does not trigger excerpt suppression; closing Reader
does not change that projection. No extra source read or hide cache is added.
Authored reply bodies are unchanged, including any quotation someone chose to write themselves.

A direct link still opens the exact withdrawn question in Reader. Its source,
body and withdrawal marker remain, as does unsent writing. Withdrawal already
settles its waiting state; this display rule adds no wait and does not reopen
one. Other questions and non-question messages retain their behavior.

Focused Node checks exercise actual grouping, head decoration, rendering,
withdrawal learning and Reader-opening owners with in-memory adapters. Native
focus, layout, gestures and Core record/wait settlement are not proved here.
